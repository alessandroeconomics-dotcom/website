Projects

Active research initiatives shaping climate and environmental policy across Brazil and Latin America.

Amazon Tipping Points: Socioeconomic Cascading Effects

Modelling the economic consequences of Amazon dieback scenarios on regional livelihoods, agriculture, and water systems across the basin.

AmazonClimate risk
2024 – ongoingFunded by: FAPESP

Brazil Voluntary Carbon Market Design

Technical advisory on regulatory frameworks for Brazil's emerging voluntary carbon market, including MRV standards and pricing mechanisms.

Carbon MarketsPolicy
2025 – ongoingFunded by: World Bank

Solar Energy Transition in the Brazilian Northeast

Assessing the macroeconomic impacts and employment effects of large-scale solar deployment across Northeast Brazil.

EnergyLabor
2023 – 2026Funded by: IDB

Climate-Smart Agriculture: Cerrado Productivity Under Warming

Panel-data analysis of how rising temperatures and shifting rainfall patterns affect soy, maize, and cotton yields in Brazil's Cerrado.

AgricultureCerrado
2024 – ongoingFunded by: EMBRAPA

CBAM Impact Assessment for Brazilian Exporters

Quantifying the trade and competitiveness effects of the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism on key Brazilian export sectors.

PolicyTrade
2025 – ongoingFunded by: CNI

Health Costs of Air Pollution from Biomass Burning

Estimating the public health burden and economic costs of wildfire and agricultural burning smoke across Brazilian states.

HealthAmazon
2024 – 2026Funded by: WHO / PAHO
